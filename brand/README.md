# Niro brand kit

The logo is a pair of joined four-pointed stars. Colours: purple `#5e17eb`; gradient `#fff6ae` (left) to `#ffaaf9` (right).

**logo/**
- `niro-logo.svg`, `niro-logo-1024.png`: the mark on purple (the main logo)
- `niro-mark.svg`, `niro-mark-1024.png`: gradient mark, transparent background (use on purple or dark)
- `niro-mark-purple.*`: one colour, for white and light backgrounds
- `niro-mark-white.*`: one colour, for dark backgrounds and photos
- `niro-lockup-on-purple.*`, `niro-lockup-on-white.*`, `niro-lockup-white-transparent.*`: mark with the word "niro" (outlined, no font needed)

**icons/**
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48), `favicon-16/32/48.png`
- `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-1024.png` (app stores)
- `maskable-512.png`: for Android, the mark sits inside the safe area so round or squircle cropping never cuts it

The app already uses these from `public/` (`favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `icons/`). Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width.
