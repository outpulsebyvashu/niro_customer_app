# Niro brand kit

Logo: the white "Niro" wordmark on a cyan radial gradient (`logo/niro-logo.svg`, `logo/niro-logo-1024.png`).
App mark: the "N" of the wordmark (`logo/niro-mark-*.svg`), used for favicons and small marks.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Brand cyan (centre of the gradient) | `#13d4fe` |
| Mid cyan | `#51dfe9` |
| Pale edge | `#cff6f7` → `#fafefe` |
| Deep ink (text, dark surfaces) | `#06222b` |
| Accent for text/links on light | `#0897b8` |
| Dark teal (partner icon) | `#0b5a6e` → `#031a21` |

## Icons per app
`icons/customer` (cyan gradient, white wordmark), `icons/partner` (dark teal, white wordmark),
`icons/workforce` (pale gradient, ink wordmark), `icons/admin` (ink, cyan wordmark).
Top-level `icons/` is the customer set. Android launcher icons live in `android/app/src/*/res/mipmap-*`.
