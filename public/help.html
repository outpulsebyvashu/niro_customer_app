Help centre

Quick answers. If yours is not here, open Niro, go to Profile and tap Contact support.

How do I sign up or log in?

Tap the person icon, enter your name and mobile number, and we text you a 6-digit code. Enter it and you are in. The same steps log you in again later.

I did not get my code.

Check the number is right and that you have signal. Wait 30 seconds and tap Resend. You can ask for up to 5 codes an hour. If it still does not arrive, use Contact support and tell us your number.

How do I earn stamps?

Open a cafe's card in Cards and show the QR code or the member code at the counter when you pay. Staff add the stamp. When the card is full, ask for your reward.

Where is my card?

Join a cafe from the map or the Home screen. Cards you join appear in Cards. Your cards stay with your account, so they are there when you log in on another phone.

Why does Niro need my location?

To show cafes near you and to alert you when you pass a partner cafe. Niro asks every time it opens. Without location it cannot open.

Why did I not get a nearby alert?

Alerts only work while Niro is open on your phone, you must be signed in, and Nearby cafe alerts must be on in Profile. Niro shows at most two a day.

Location is blocked. How do I fix it?

Tap the lock icon next to the address bar, set Location to Allow, then tap Ask again in Niro.

How do I save an offer?

Tap Save on any offer on the Home screen. Find your saved offers in Profile, then Saved offers.

How do I change my name, birthday or photo?

Profile, then Edit. Birthday and special days let cafes send you a treat if you leave treats on.

Can I put Niro on my home screen?

Yes. In Chrome tap the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. On iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How do I download or delete my data?

Profile, then Download my data gives you a file with everything we hold about you. Delete my data removes your account for good.

Which cafes are on Niro?

Only partner cafes that have joined. If none show near you yet, none have joined in your area. Ask your favourite cafe to join.

Still stuck?

Open Niro, tap the person icon, then Contact support, and write to us. Please include your mobile number if you cannot sign in.
