Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Niro is a digital loyalty card. You collect stamps at partner cafes and restaurants, and Niro can tell you when you are near one. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain words on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account: your mobile number and name. We use the number to verify it is you (we text you a one-time code).
  • Optional profile details: email, birthday, special days, food preference, usual order and a profile photo. You choose whether to add any of these.
  • Loyalty activity: the cafes you join, your member code, stamps, rewards and the history of both, and offers you save.
  • Location: your phone shares its location with the Niro page while it is open. We use it to show cafes near you and to decide when to show a nearby alert. Your approximate coordinates are sent to our server to find nearby cafes and to name your area. We do not keep a location history. We only record that a nearby alert was shown (which cafe, and when) so we can keep to the limit of two a day.
  • Messages to support: what you write, and the contact detail you give us.
  • Technical data: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 days. Your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (for example, too many code requests). It is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form with support messages.

How we use it

  • To run your account and your loyalty cards.
  • To show nearby cafes, offers and alerts.
  • To keep Niro safe and stop misuse.
  • To answer your messages.

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Who else sees it

  • Cafes you join can see that you are a member, your name, and your card activity (stamps and rewards). They can send you offers only if you left offers switched on for that cafe, and you can switch that off in Profile at any time.
  • Cloudflare hosts Niro and stores our database.
  • Our SMS provider (StartMessaging) receives your mobile number and the code so it can deliver the text.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map. Your phone loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap, so it can see your IP address and which area of the map you view. Our server also asks OpenStreetMap for the name of your area, using rounded coordinates, without your name or number.
  • We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. One-time codes expire after 5 minutes. Sign-in sessions last 30 days. When you delete your data, your profile, cards, stamps, saved offers and nearby-alert records are removed.

Your choices

  • See and fix your details: Profile, then Edit.
  • Download your data: Profile, then Download my data. You get one file with everything we hold about you.
  • Delete your data: Profile, then Delete my data. This cannot be undone.
  • Nearby alerts: switch them off in Profile. You can also block location for this site in your browser settings, but then Niro cannot open.
  • Questions or complaints: use Profile, then Contact support. We will reply as soon as we can. You also have the rights given by Indian data protection law, including access, correction and erasure.

Security

Codes are stored only in scrambled form and are limited to a few tries. Sign-in cookies are protected and sent only over a secure connection. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your phone locked.

Children

Niro is for people aged 18 and over. If you think a child has given us their details, contact support and we will delete them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
